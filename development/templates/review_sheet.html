Tile review sheet

What to do. For each tile, look at the photo and say what you see — ignore what the software thought. Fine: no visible damage. Chipped: a piece of the corner or edge is missing. Cracked: a visible crack line on the surface. Both, or Unsure if you can't tell (unsure tiles are left out of the maths). Click a photo to enlarge it. Keys: 1 fine  2 chipped  3 cracked  4 both  5 unsure  ←→ move  Backspace clear. The tiles most worth your time are first; even the first 20 are useful. Answers save in this browser as you go. When you finish (or stop), press Export labels and give me the file.
