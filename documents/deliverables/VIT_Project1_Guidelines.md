## BMEE497J/BMHA497J Project 1 Guidelines

Source: guidelines email from the college (received 2026-07-10). Kept verbatim here as
the reference document; per-review folders in `deliverables/` extract the specific
requirements and due dates relevant to this project.

### 1. Project Type and Team Size

- Project may be a theoretical analysis, modelling & simulation, experimentation & analysis, prototype design, fabrication of new equipment, correlation and analysis of data, software development, applied research and any other related activities.
- Project must be related to the field of specialization of the student.
- Projects may be undertaken individually or as a group, with a maximum of three students per team.
- For group projects, each student must submit an individual project report, clearly specifying their personal contribution to the overall work.

### 2. Guidance and Faculty Eligibility

- Faculty members may guide projects only within their area of expertise.
- Students must not approach faculty outside SMEC, unless the project is a verified interdisciplinary project involving students from multiple branches.
- A faculty member may guide a maximum of four B.Tech. projects, irrespective of the number of students in each batch.
- The assigned guide will take full responsibility for supervising project execution and will assist in evaluating student performance.

### 3. Expected Project Outcomes

- Students must aim to achieve at least one major outcome, such as:
  - A prototype/model, or
  - A research publication, or
  - A patent filing.
- Any prototype developed must be submitted to the school upon completion of the project.

### 4. Research and Publication Encouragement

- Students are encouraged to publish their work in peer-reviewed journals or present at international conferences, which will be considered an added advantage during evaluation.

### 5. Logbook Maintenance

- Students must maintain a daily logbook documenting all project-related activities.
- The logbook must be signed regularly by the guide.
- Students must produce the logbook upon request or during any of the scheduled review processes.
