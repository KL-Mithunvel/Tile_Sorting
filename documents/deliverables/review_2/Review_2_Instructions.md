# Review 2 — Report and Submission Instructions

Source: email to students, received 2026-10-12, "Review 2 panel details for B.Tech. projects
guided by SMEC faculty" (panel list attached to the original email, not reproduced here).
Saved verbatim in substance; the checklist at the bottom is derived.

## 1. Project report preparation and submission

- **Report format** — use the template for your degree programme. If a group mixes
  branches, each student follows their own branch's format, including cover page and
  initial pages. Fix the highlighted text on the front pages and **remove the highlighting**.
- **Originality** — original work only; **no plagiarised or AI-generated content**.
- **Printing and binding** — A4, **soft-bound with black tape binding**.
- **Copies** — at least one. Normally **N + 2** (N = students in the group): one per
  student, one for the guide, one for the department. (3 students -> 5 copies.)
- **Guide's signature** — obtain on the report **before** the review.
- **Examiners' signatures** — obtained on the report **during** the review; submit the
  reports at the review venue.
- **HOD signature and soft copy** — HODs sign the reports; collect from the School Office
  after one or two days. Photograph the certificate page with all signatures, insert it
  into the soft copy, and submit the final PDF through **VTOP**.

## 2. Documents required during Review 2

All students must:

- Get the guide's approval and signature on the **Evaluation Form** and the **Report**
  well in advance.
- Submit the **signed Evaluation Form** during the review session.
- Bring and submit the **Project Log Book** (records of meetings, discussions and progress
  with the guide) — see `../logbook/`.
- Prepare the presentation against the **evaluation rubrics** (see `README.md` here).

## 3. Important

- Sessions may **not** follow the panel-list order. Be present for the **whole** scheduled
  slot/day and ready to present when called.
- Complete all report formalities well in advance.

## Checklist

- [ ] Report on the correct branch template; highlighting removed from front pages
- [ ] Content is own work — no AI-generated text (see note below)
- [ ] Guide signature on report
- [ ] Guide signature on Evaluation Form
- [ ] Project Log Book up to date and guide-signed (`../logbook/Logbook.md`)
- [ ] Printed, A4, black tape soft-bound; 5 copies (3 students + guide + department)
- [ ] Presentation prepared against the rubric
- [ ] After review: HOD signature collected, certificate page photographed, final PDF on VTOP

## Note for this repo

The "no AI-generated content" rule applies to the **submitted report**. Anything drafted
with an assistant in this repo (including `../review_1/Review_1_Content.md`) should be
rewritten in the team's own words before it goes into the report.
